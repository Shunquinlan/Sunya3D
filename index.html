A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we
get making.

Something else on your mind?
Ask us directly

Do I need a finished 3D model?+

No. A sketch, a few photos, measurements or a clear description is enough to start the conversation. CAD design can be included in your project or requested on its own.

Can you recreate a broken part?+

Often, yes. Share photos, dimensions and how the part is used. We’ll assess whether a printed replacement is appropriate and discuss material and fit. High-load, high-heat or safety-critical uses require a separate suitability review.

What files can I send?+

STL and 3MF are useful for print projects; STEP is useful for editable CAD geometry. Photos, sketches and drawings are welcome too. Add a shared file link to your brief or attach files to your email.

How much will my project cost?+

Each quote depends on design time, size, material, quantity, printing time and any assembly or finishing. We review the project with you before agreeing on a price. There’s no automatic charge when you send an inquiry.

Can you help with electronics as well?+

Yes. Tell us about your gadget or prototype, including the components, connections and what you want it to do. We can discuss custom enclosures, mounting, access and electronics integration within the project scope.

How long does a project take?+

It depends on complexity, design revisions, material availability and the print schedule. Include your target date in the brief so we can confirm a realistic timeline before work begins.
